# Research: UI/UX Design

Pass 1 of the niche research. Scouted 25 Nov 2026.

**Method.** Candidates came from recent roundups (Colorlib's live-checked 2026 list, UXPin, CareerFoundry) plus a search for Nigerian designers. Every site below was opened in the last 24 hours. Sites marked *(screenshot)* were rendered in a browser and looked at. The rest were read as page text, so their visual notes are limited to what the text shows. Five candidates were dropped: two dead or failing (kelvinedimeh.me, danielabayomi.com), one blocked (simonpan.com), and two that are better saved for other niches (Katie McCurdy and Julia Costa for UX Research).

**Use.** Structure and layout inspiration only. Never copy anyone's design, code, images or text. Testimonials and metrics on these sites belong to their owners.

## Patterns

**First screen (what the best ones put above the fold)**
1. Name, role and one-line positioning in plain language.
2. Availability: "Open to full-time and contract", with a green dot or a ticker (Layor, Zoë, Ellen, Oluwakemi).
3. Two actions at most: usually Resume/CV and Email (copy-to-clipboard is popular), or Book a call for freelancers.
4. Social proof early: logos, a stats row or a short testimonial.

**Project cards.** The strongest cards answer three questions without a click: what was the problem, what was my role, what changed. Common fields: company or context label, outcome-led title or question title, one-line summary, role, 1 to 2 metrics, tags, image.

**Case study structure.** Context → problem → my role and team → process (research, explorations, decisions and why) → solution → outcome (numbers if real, honest limits if not) → what I'd do next. NDA work gets a one-line outcome and "available on request".

**Trust.** References from managers, client logos, honest limits ("a three-person test"), labelled practice work for juniors.

**Personality.** One personal element is enough: art, hobbies, a photo strip, a live clock. Always below or beside the work, never instead of it.

**Contact.** Email visible or one-click copy, LinkedIn, CV. Freelancers add booking links. None of the 20 offer WhatsApp; for Nigerian and African users that is a clear gap Forma should fill.

**Seniority shapes the layout.** Juniors: fewer projects, process-heavy, honest labelling. Seniors: outcomes, systems, leadership, references. Freelancers: services, pricing and booking.

## Required sections for the interview agent (UI/UX Design)

The interview should collect, in this order, and skip anything already known:

1. **Identity:** full name, what you want to be called, role title, city and country.
2. **Positioning:** one line on what you design and for whom (the AI drafts it from answers, the user approves).
3. **Status:** open to full-time, contract, freelance, internships, or not looking. Remote or on-site.
4. **Seniority:** years of experience, or "just starting" (switches the template set and copy tone).
5. **Projects (2 to 4):** for each one, name, company or "personal/practice project", your role, team and timeline, the problem, 2 or 3 key decisions, the result (numbers only if the user gives them), images or a Figma/prototype link, NDA yes/no.
6. **Skills and tools:** methods (research, interaction, visual, prototyping, design systems, accessibility) and tools (Figma, etc.).
7. **Experience:** companies, titles, years. Optional for juniors (offer education or training instead, e.g. a bootcamp).
8. **Proof (optional):** testimonials, clients or logos, awards. Never invented.
9. **Extras (optional):** writing, talks, side projects, art or hobbies.
10. **Contact:** email, WhatsApp number, LinkedIn, Dribbble or Behance, CV upload, booking link for freelancers.

**Required sections on the page:** hero (name, role, positioning, status), work (cards plus case study pages), about, skills and tools, experience (or education/training for juniors), contact. **Niche-specific:** case study pages, availability status, CV download, optional testimonials, optional process section (recommended for juniors).

## Notes for later phases

- **Interview agent:** ask for the result of each project as "what changed after it shipped?" and accept "I don't know yet". Never fill in numbers.
- **Builder:** generate outcome-led card titles from the user's own result answer. Offer a question-style title as an alternative.
- **SEO (Phase 11):** target phrases like "UI/UX designer in Lagos", "product designer portfolio", plus the user's name and city.
