**How this differs.** Renders and turntables are the proof; recruiters (games, film, product viz, archviz) want wireframes, topology, textures and software breakdowns.

## Patterns
- Full-bleed renders and loops; grid of thumbnails labelled by discipline (character, environment, product, archviz, abstract).
- Breakdown pages: clay render, wireframe, texture maps, lighting passes, final.
- Tools listed (Blender, Cinema 4D, Maya, ZBrush, Substance, Houdini, Unreal, Redshift, Octane).
- ArtStation-style focus on single pieces; studios show clients and roles.

## Required sections for the interview agent
1. Identity, city; specialty (characters, environments, hard-surface, product viz, archviz, abstract/loops, NFTs).
2. Pieces (6 to 20): title, image/video, type, software, client or personal, breakdown images (optional), year.
3. Tools; clients/studios; awards.
4. Contact: email, WhatsApp, ArtStation, Instagram, Behance.
