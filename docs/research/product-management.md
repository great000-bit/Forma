## Patterns

- **Case anatomy:** context → problem and evidence → options → prioritisation and trade-offs → what shipped → outcome metrics → what I learned.
- **Artefacts:** PRDs (redacted), roadmaps, experiment readouts, teardowns, strategy memos.
- **Writing** as proof (blogs, essays). Leadership PMs show team and process.
- **Beginners:** product teardowns, personal products, simulated case studies clearly labelled.

## Required sections for the interview agent (Product Management)

1. Identity: name, title (APM, PM, Senior, Group, Head), domain, city, remote.
2. Products and domains (fintech, health, SaaS, consumer).
3. Case studies (2 to 4): context, problem, evidence, options, decision, what shipped, outcome (only real numbers), your role vs team.
4. Artefacts (optional, redacted): PRD, roadmap, experiment.
5. Skills: discovery, analytics, experimentation, stakeholder management, technical depth.
6. Experience; education/MBA; certifications.
7. Writing, talks, side products (optional).
8. Contact: email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, CV.
