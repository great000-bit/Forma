## Patterns

- **Video first:** autoplay muted reel or full-screen header; embedded YouTube/Vimeo for each piece.
- **Categories:** commercials, music videos, YouTube, documentary, weddings, corporate, social (short-form).
- **Commercial mechanics:** packages and tiers, booking forms, turnaround times, client logos, testimonials.
- **Creator economy:** tutorials, presets/LUT shop, newsletter.
- **Credits:** director, editor, colourist, client; editors should state exactly what they cut, graded or mixed.

## Required sections for the interview agent (Video Editing)

1. Identity: name, title (editor, colourist, editor and motion), city, remote.
2. Formats: YouTube long-form, short-form (Reels/TikTok/Shorts), commercials, music videos, documentary, weddings, corporate.
3. Status and packages: freelance/full-time, turnaround times, starting prices (optional, in naira or USD).
4. Reel link.
5. Projects (4 to 10): title, client or channel, format, your role (edit, colour, sound, motion), link, thumbnail, results (views, retention, subscribers) only if the user gives them.
6. Software: Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut, After Effects, CapCut.
7. Clients and testimonials (optional).
8. Contact: email, WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, booking.

**Page sections:** hero with reel, work by format, project pages (video + credits), services/packages, about, contact. **Niche-specific:** format filters, role-per-project labels, packages block, optional results chips.
