**How this differs.** Identity designers sell systems, not single logos: strategy, logo suite, type, colour, guidelines, rollout across touchpoints, and the client's business context.

## Patterns (from analysed identity portfolios)
- Full-width client panels; case studies open with the client's problem.
- Each case: brief → strategy/positioning → concepts → final mark → system (type, colour, grid) → applications (signage, packaging, social, web) → guidelines.
- Concept titles over client names for some; credits for agency work.
- Services and process pages for studios; inquiry forms asking budget and timeline.

## Required sections for the interview agent
1. Identity: name or studio, city.
2. Services: strategy, naming, logo, visual identity, guidelines, packaging, rebrands.
3. Cases (3 to 6): client, industry, brief, your role, strategy line, final mark, system elements, applications (images), year.
4. Process (optional), pricing from (optional), clients and testimonials.
5. Contact: email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Behance, inquiry form (budget, timeline).
