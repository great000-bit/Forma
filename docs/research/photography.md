## Patterns

- **Full-bleed images**, minimal chrome, short menus.
- **Captions:** client, publication, location, credits.
- **Business layer:** pricing or "from" price, packages, FAQ, booking, quote button, licensing and usage terms, proofing.
- **Galleries by service** (weddings, family, product) or by client type.
- **Smart contact forms:** date, location, use, budget, number of images.
- **Local SEO:** city and service names in page titles (corporate events London, etc.).

## Required sections for the interview agent (Photography)

1. Identity: name, city, areas served, travel.
2. Specialties: portrait, wedding, events, family, product, food, fashion, real estate/architecture, sports, documentary, wildlife.
3. Packages and prices (optional, naira/USD), booking lead time.
4. Galleries (2 to 6): name, 6 to 30 photos, client or event, location, credits.
5. Clients, publications, awards (optional).
6. Testimonials (optional).
7. Prints/licensing (optional).
8. Contact: email, WhatsApp, Instagram, booking form fields (date, location, type).

**Page sections:** full-bleed hero, galleries, about, services/pricing, testimonials, contact with booking form. **Niche-specific:** gallery engine (grid, masonry, slideshow), lightbox, image optimisation (AVIF/WebP, lazy load), booking form fields, pricing table.
