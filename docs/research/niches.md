## Proposed additional niches (missing from the PRD list)

1. **Data Engineering** — distinct from data science; 6 of the 24 data portfolios were data engineers.
2. **Content Design / UX Writing** — appeared in UI/UX research (Andrew Webb) and copywriting; a recognised hiring track.
3. **Developer Relations / Developer Education** — writing, talks, sample apps; overlaps tech writing but hired separately.
4. **Digital Marketing / SEO** — large freelance market in Nigeria; sits beside social media management.
5. **Sound Design / Music Production for media** — appears in motion and game credits; strong creative market in Nigeria.
6. **Content Creation (YouTube/TikTok creators)** — creators need media kits more than portfolios; strong local demand.
7. **Community Management** — distinct from social media management in tech companies.
8. **Robotics** — currently folded into Embedded/IoT; Nigerian portfolios (e.g. Robinson Honour) list it separately.

## Cross-niche rules learned (apply to every template)

- First screen answers: who, what, availability, how to contact (email copy, WhatsApp, CV).
- Cards carry context (client/company, role, year) and, where real, one result.
- Honest labels: practice/spec work, NDA "on request", sanitised data, synthetic data.
- Two audiences: employers (CV, experience) and clients (services, packages, booking).
- WhatsApp contact was absent from almost every scouted portfolio — Forma's clearest local advantage.
- Low-data mode: posters before video, lazy embeds, compressed images.
