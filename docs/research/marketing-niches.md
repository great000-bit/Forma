## Copywriting
**Proof:** samples by format (web pages, landing pages, emails, ads, product descriptions, scripts, UX copy), before/after rewrites, results (conversion, CTR, sales — only real), clients and industries, testimonials.
**Required sections:** specialty (conversion, brand voice, email, SEO, UX writing, scripts); samples (client, format, brief, the copy or link, result if given); industries; services and rates (optional); testimonials; contact.
**Rules:** clients' confidential copy marked "used with permission" or rewritten as spec work and labelled.
