## Patterns

- **Reel first.** 45 to 90 seconds, on or one click from the first screen. Strongest: a timestamped breakdown with client, agency and role per shot.
- **Credits everywhere:** client, studio or agency, year, exact role (design, animation, direction), tools, team.
- **Loops over text.** Autoplaying, muted, short loops sell style faster than copy. Must have poster images and lazy loading for slow networks.
- **Process:** styleframes, boards, design quilts, experiments logs.
- **Freelance mechanics:** availability ticker, services menu, rates FAQ, booking.
- **Product motion variant:** link to the shipped product, not just the video.

## Required sections for the interview agent (Motion Design)

1. Identity: name, title, city, studio or in-house.
2. Specialty: 2D, 3D, character, titles, UI/product motion, motion branding, explainers.
3. Status: freelance (available from), full-time, booked.
4. Reel: upload or link (YouTube, Vimeo), length, optional breakdown (timestamp, client, role).
5. Projects (4 to 8): title, client, agency or studio, year, your exact role, tools, the film (link), 3 to 6 stills or loops, optional styleframes and process.
6. Tools: After Effects, Cinema 4D, Blender, Houdini, Cavalry, Lottie, Rive, Redshift, etc.
7. Clients and studios (optional), awards (optional).
8. Contact: email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Vimeo, LinkedIn, rates note (optional).

**Page sections:** hero with reel, work grid of loops, project pages (film + credits + stills), about (tools, clients), contact. **Niche-specific:** reel player with optional breakdown, credits block, loop thumbnails with poster fallback, availability ticker.
