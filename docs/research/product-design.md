Pass 2. Scouted 25 Nov 2026.

**Method.** Sources: SiteBuilderReport's product designer list (updated May 2026), UXPin's 2026 list, Colorlib's live-checked 2026 UX list. **Opened** means the page was read today. **Roundup** means details come from the roundup and the site was not opened by us. Inspiration for structure only; never copy designs, code, images or text.

**How this differs from UI/UX.** Product designers are hired for judgement across the whole product: strategy, trade-offs, cross-team work and business outcomes. Their portfolios show fewer screens and more decisions.

## Patterns

- **Hero:** name, level, current company or focus, years, city. One or two actions (Resume and Email, or Book a call).
- **Cards:** outcome-led title, company or industry label, engagement type, year, role.
- **Case studies:** context, goal and success measures, constraints, options considered, decision and why, result, what's next. Senior portfolios add team, influence and stakeholder stories.
- **Status signals:** "booked until Q3", "project in flight", "case study locked, ask me".
- **Extras that work:** teaching, writing, side projects shipped, a garden or playground for small experiments.
- **Physical product variant:** materials, dimensions, manufacturing, exhibitions and shop.

## Required sections for the interview agent (Product Design)

1. Identity: name, title, level, city, remote or on-site.
2. Positioning: what kinds of products and users (e.g. fintech for small businesses).
3. Status and engagement types: full-time, contract, freelance, booked until.
4. Digital or physical products (switches the field set).
5. Projects (2 to 4): context, goal, how success was measured, constraints, your role and team, key decisions and why, result (only if given), what you'd do next, NDA yes/no, images or prototype links. Physical: materials, dimensions, process photos.
6. Experience: companies, roles, years.
7. Skills: strategy, research, interaction, visual, prototyping, systems, front-end.
8. Proof (optional): testimonials, logos, talks, writing, mentoring.
9. Contact: email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, CV, booking link.

**Page sections:** hero, work, case study pages, about, experience, skills, contact. **Niche-specific:** engagement status, success-measure block on each case, optional side projects or garden, optional shop (physical).
