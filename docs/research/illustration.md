## Patterns

- **Route by buyer:** portfolios split by market (editorial, publishing, brand, kids, medical), each with its own contact or agent.
- **Captions:** client and job on every piece.
- **Commerce is normal:** shops, prints, Patreon, licensing, stock. Separate commission email from shop orders.
- **Availability banners** and "next available" dates.
- **Freshness:** news logs, latest piece first.
- **Style consistency** across the grid is itself the pitch.

## Required sections for the interview agent (Illustration)

1. Identity: name, city, agent (optional).
2. Markets: editorial, picture books, brand/packaging, character, maps, medical/scientific, murals, lettering, kids' media, comics.
3. Status: commissions open, next available date.
4. Work (8 to 24 pieces): image, title, client (or personal), use (cover, packaging, editorial), year, market tag.
5. Books (optional): covers, titles, publishers.
6. Shop/prints/Patreon links (optional).
7. Awards, exhibitions, clients (optional).
8. Contact: email (commissions), agent, WhatsApp, Instagram, Behance.

**Page sections:** hero (one strong piece or grid), work by market, about, clients, contact. **Niche-specific:** market filters, client captions, agent routing, shop link, availability banner.
